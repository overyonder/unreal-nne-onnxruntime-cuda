# Third-party notices

The installation script downloads ONNX Runtime 1.24.3 from its official NuGet package. ONNX Runtime is licensed under the MIT License. Its source and license are available at <https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/tree/v1.24.3>. The pinned license and upstream third-party notice texts are installed under `ThirdPartyLicenses` and staged with the plugin.

The script also downloads NVIDIA CUDA 12.8 and cuDNN 9.8 runtime packages. Their pinned license texts are installed under `ThirdPartyLicenses` and staged with the plugin.
